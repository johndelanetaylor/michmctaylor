## packaging refresh & product expansion

Following Stem Ciders' acquisition of Howdy Beer from The Post Brewing, I collaborated with the marketing team to revamp Howdy's 12 oz can, redesign the 6-pack wrap, and develop new artwork for 16 oz cans.

## logo refresh

An overhaul to the Howdy logo was needed – I meticulously redrew the custom HOWDY lettering to have clean anchor points and consistent slabs and corners, refined the typography throughout, and restored symmetry to the badge and horseshoe elements. I extended the branding's versatility by creating additional logotypes, marks, and various iterations for seamless integration across web and merchandise applications. All of these changes aid in better legibility and longevity.
